Preview the 3-email welcome sequence

Drafted in Eli’s voice, for his review.

1. Welcome. Here’s how this works.Thanks for joining. Healing Habits is built on one idea: mental health and personal growth aren’t only things we talk about. They’re things we practice. Over the next few emails, I’ll share how we think about change and give you something small to try.
2. Knowing it and doing it aren’t the same thing.You probably already know a lot about what you’d like to change. Insight matters. It just doesn’t do the work for you. So here’s the question I ask: okay, we understand what needs to change. What are we actually going to do about it this week?
3. Your first practice.Pick one thing you’ve been avoiding because it feels uncomfortable. Make it small enough to finish this week. Name the barrier ahead of time. Then do it and notice what happens. Want help building a plan? The first conversation is free: book a 20-minute consultation.
